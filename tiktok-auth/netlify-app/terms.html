What this is

This is a self-hosted automation tool that generates vertical short videos and posts them to TikTok via the TikTok Content Posting API. You run it on your own machine, with your own credentials, and you remain in control of everything it publishes.

How it works

The tool uses the following third-party services as part of its workflow:

  • Groq — generates phrases and the caption text.
  • Pexels — provides background images.
  • ffmpeg and Pillow — render the video locally on your machine.
  • TikTok — publishes the finished video via the Content Posting API.
