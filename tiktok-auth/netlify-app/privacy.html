Privacy Policy

What data this app handles

  • The theme input you provide.
  • Generated phrases and caption text (produced by Groq).
  • Background images (provided by Pexels).
  • The rendered .mp4 file, which is created and kept locally on your machine.
  • TikTok OAuth tokens, stored in tiktok_tokens.json on your machine.

What we do not collect

There is no analytics, no tracking pixels, and no telemetry. Nothing about your usage is reported back to the authors of this tool.

Third-party services that receive data

ServiceData it receives
Groq The theme and prompts used to generate phrases and the caption.
Pexels The image query used to find background images.
TikTok The video and caption, sent via the Content Posting API.
Netlify Hosts this OAuth callback page only. It receives the authorization code, exchanges it for tokens using your app’s client secret, and returns the tokens to your browser in the snippet shown on screen.

Where data is stored

API keys are stored locally in segredos.txt and OAuth tokens in tiktok_tokens.json. Both files live on your machine and are gitignored, so they are never committed to version control.

Your rights

  • You can delete the local files at any time.
  • You can revoke this app’s access from TikTok Settings > Security > Apps.
  • You can rotate your API keys whenever you want.
